This is a **sales discovery** call. If there's a concrete, advanceable outcome (a committed next step, a scoped pilot, a qualified/disqualified decision), lock it as the goal — but don't force one. Shape the **direction** toward a qualification stance: explore the prospect's current solution and its pain, the frequency/cost of that pain, budget, decision authority, timeline, and the wedge where the product fits — staying in listen-and-qualify mode, not pitching. Only suggest a checklist item for a concrete must-verify fact the user names (e.g. "Current Snowflake spend", "Who signs off on budget"). Push back on vague directions like "see if there's a fit".
